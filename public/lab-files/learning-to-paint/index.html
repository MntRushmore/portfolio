A tiny neural network, written from scratch and trained right here in your browser. It is a function from a position (x, y) to a colour (r, g, b). It never stores the picture: each step it looks at 256 random pixels from a 64×64 copy, guesses their colours, and nudges its weights to guess better. Pause it and it renders at full resolution: because it learned a function, not pixels, it can be drawn at any size.
